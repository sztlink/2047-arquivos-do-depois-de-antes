# Voice construction: an empirical, iterative method

> **Historical note (October 2026).** Written during the research and rehearsal phase of the work, then called *Projeto 2047 / Arquivo Terra Viva*, and kept as process. Some of it no longer describes the work as it runs. For the current apparatus see `../03-field-notes/2026-09-restart-from-zero-and-the-2047-structure.md` and `../03-field-notes/2026-10-opening-at-casa-firjan.md`.

**Status:** method validated; voice settled in co-authorial reading (July 2026).

The novel is written in a voice built from the co-author's own prose, with her explicit consent. Early on we rejected the abstract route (a "cartography of the voice" defined on paper) in favor of an empirical loop: generate, read together, lapidate, settle.

## The loop

1. **Corpus with clearance.** A voice corpus is curated from material the co-author explicitly provided for this purpose. The corpus itself is never published and never quoted by the machine.
2. **Profile: what crosses, what stays.** The corpus is distilled into a profile that separates what transfers to fiction (rhythm of breath and thrust, torsion-as-recusal, productive paradox held without reconciliation, the movement from abstract to concrete, hard unconsoling closures) from what is essay apparatus and must not migrate (argumentative scaffolding, reader-facing address, hedges, real cultural references cited as evidence, quotable authorial tics).
3. **Dosage versions.** The same opening chapter is generated in several versions, each a different dosage of the profile (thrust-dominant, breath-dominant, body-dominant, memory-inverted, forensic-control).
4. **Co-authorial reading.** The versions go to the co-author for reading, comments, and inline edits. Nothing is attributed to her until she ratifies.
5. **Lapidation harvested, not guessed.** Her comments and suggested edits are harvested programmatically and read closely, including what she chose *not* to change. The finest calibration rules come from that contrast.
6. **Settled voice contract.** The result is a behavioral contract the machine must obey in every chapter.

## What settled (July 2026)

The memory-inverted voice won: the novel is narrated from later inside 2047, the present told as remembrance.

The settled contract is a short list of small enforceable behaviors, punctuation first among them, that the coauthor marked on the page and gave as written recipes. Those rules are part of the coauthor's authorship. They govern every chapter and are not published in this archive.

The larger lesson stands: a synthetic voice is not settled by adjectives ("literary", "essayistic") but by small enforceable behaviors that a human author actually marked on the page.
