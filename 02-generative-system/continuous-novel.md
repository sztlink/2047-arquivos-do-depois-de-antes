# From fragments to a continuous novel

**Status:** settled decision (July 2026).  
**Supersedes:** the story-packet as the dramaturgical unit. The packet survives as a transport and ledger unit; it no longer defines what the machine writes.

## The decision

The work does not write self-contained fragments that react to each news item. It writes **one continuous novel set in 2047**, in chapters, over the whole duration of the exhibition and beyond.

This changes the machine's dramaturgy:

- **Persistent cast.** Characters recur and develop over time. The current cast, ratified in co-authorial reading: a water-compliance verifier, a man from the lower village who still knows the old path to the spring, and the Archive's Voice, a narrator writing from later inside the same year 2047.
- **An arc that advances.** Every chapter must move something. The machine is not allowed to reset the world between chapters.
- **News as pressure, not headline.** The rare-earth news the machine reads is metabolized as material pressure inside the story (water that changes owner, a polygon that gains a licensee, a spreadsheet cell that does not close). The news item is never the subject of the chapter and never appears as a headline.

## Narrator position

The novel is narrated from later inside 2047: the present of the story is told as something already remembered. This memory inversion is the voice's critical instrument. It denaturalizes the present by treating it as the past of a very near future.

## Governance constraints (hard rules)

- Never stage indigenous or quilombola voices in first person. Figures adjacent to those histories appear only as anonymous presences, never ventriloquized.
- No real people, no brands.
- No futuristic gadgets. 2047 is rendered through licenses, spreadsheets, drainage, paper and titles, not through devices.
- Style is never content: the voice carries the writing, but the co-author's essayistic arguments and named references do not migrate into the fiction.

## Where the text lives

The novel text itself is not published in this repository. It accumulates in the paper archive printed by the apparatus, and is reserved for the work's own formats. This repository documents the process that produces it.
