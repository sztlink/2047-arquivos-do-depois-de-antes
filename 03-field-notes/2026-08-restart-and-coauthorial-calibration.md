# Field note . August 2026 . The novel restarts, and the machine goes back to school

Status. Field note, public and sanitized. Dates are real, internals are summarized.

## The decision

On August 6 the novel restarted from chapter 1. Everything generated before that date, including one hundred and five chapters printed during operational rehearsals, was reclassified as rehearsal material. It remains archived, and it no longer counts as the work.

The trigger was a close reading by the coauthor. Her annotations on the early chapters were distilled, comment by comment, into a revised rule set for the writing engine. Half of the annotations were praise. The other half repeated a small number of structural complaints so consistently that they stopped being notes and became specification.

## What the reading taught the engine

Four corrections, each now enforced by the generation pipeline rather than by good intentions.

The world's private vocabulary must be introduced once, concretely, before the prose can use it as shorthand. A reader cannot inherit the authors' familiarity.

Anachronism is a test, not a list. Any object or interface imported from the office culture of the 1990s to 2020s must be replaced by its 2047 equivalent, and the forbidden-words list is only an example of the error.

The antagonist must have a name. Power that hides behind a generic word for the system is a cliché, and the engine now refuses it. Someone signs, someone profits, someone operates.

Abstraction only exists embodied. What the prose claims, it must make happen in a body, an object, a quantity, an hour.

## The premise sharpened

The research corpus that feeds the machine caught up with the news. Datacenter construction is no longer a distant fact the valley only feels through demand. Campuses are being negotiated onto the same territories that supply the minerals, courted by public incentive, promising closed loops, and asking for the same water the mines already dispute. The novel's world premise was rewritten to hold both ends of the chain on the same ground.

## The calibration loop

The rewrite is not a one-off. The novel is now written inside a coauthorial loop, in which the coauthor's reading of each batch becomes writing directives for the next, and silence is recorded too. The internal routine of the loop stays outside this archive.

The loop ended in a deeper restart on August 22. See `2026-09-restart-from-zero-and-the-2047-structure.md`.

## What this changes in the archive

The printed rehearsal chapters keep their QR trails and their paper. They are the record of a machine learning to write, which is part of the work's subject. The novel itself begins again at chapter one, with a world bible, a character census, and a phase structure the rehearsals did not have.
