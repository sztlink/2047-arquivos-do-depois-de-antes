# Field note . August to September 2026 . Restart from zero, and a book of 2047 chapters

Status. Field note, public and sanitized. Dates are real, internals are summarized.

The August 6 note described a restart from chapter 1 inside a daily calibration loop. That loop ran, and it ended in a deeper restart.

## August 22. From zero, from the apparatus

After a close reading of the first fifty-five chapters, the two authors closed the plan of a hundred chapters at fifty-five and restarted the novel from zero. Nothing written before survives into the book.

Restart from zero has a precise meaning in the pipeline. A new chapter is born only from the apparatus: the master instruction, the canon of the world, the cast, and the score. An old chapter never enters the prompt. The earlier corpus remains useful only as training and rule material, never as text to continue.

The master instruction of the same day named the narrator, Herética Ablation, and reorganized the novel in four parts. Companies in the body of the text are fictional. The real ones appear only where the page is documentary, in the source line at the top of each chapter.

## September 5. The writing moves

The local writing path, an open model with a voice adapter (see `2026-08-voice-trained-and-deployed.md`), left the production line. Chapters are written by a cloud model, GPT-5.6 by OpenAI, because the quality of its prose is the one Giselle Beiguelman holds for the book. In October the local writer became legacy.

The rest of the generative chain stays local. A small local model, Qwen2.5-3B, triages the news. The English edition of each chapter is translated by Claude and published only when the original it was made from is unchanged.

The local adapter and the notes on training it remain in this archive as process. See `../02-generative-system/model-voice-and-training.md`, `../02-generative-system/fine-tuning-vs-rag.md`, and `../02-generative-system/corpus-policy.md`.

## September 16. Eighty-nine notebooks of twenty-three

The novel received its arithmetic. **89 notebooks of 23 chapters, 89 times 23 equals 2047.** One notebook fills one day of the open room, so the book has a calendar and the calendar has an end. The narrative ends at chapter 2047.

## Four vertices

Each chapter is anchored in one real news item, and each item is filed under one vertex of the chain the work is about.

- **Extracted territory**, the mined body.
- **Processing power**, who governs the flow.
- **Western market**.
- **Datacenter and water bridge**, the fourth vertex, added when datacenter construction began to compete for the same water as the mines.

A collector reads news feeds and searches through the day. By October 20, 2026 the public source bank held 3512 items, each linked to its origin, at <https://heretica.xyz/banco-de-dados.html>.

## September 23. Operation as procedure

An experiment with three parallel runs settled a directive. The machine is asked for a procedure, not for a theme. A chapter is an operation carried out in the world of 2047, with a body, an object, a quantity, and an hour, and not an essay about the present.

## September 28. The base of the season

The sixteenth run became the base of the exhibition season. Its first 115 chapters are frozen. From there the exhibition machine writes notebooks ahead of the printer, by itself, up to one hundred and thirty-eight chapters ahead of what is already on paper. The engine also proposes the two video scenes and the character sheets of each chapter. Human approval of each scene and sheet was removed on purpose: the proposal belongs to the engine.

## What did not change

The voice contract settled with the coauthor still governs every chapter. Its rules are part of her authorship and are not published here.
