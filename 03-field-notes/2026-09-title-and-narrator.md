# Field note . August to October 2026 . The title, and the narrator who signs the book

Status. Field note, public and sanitized. Dates are real.

## From working title to title

The project was born as *Projeto 2047 / Arquivo Terra Viva*. It was always a working title, a name for the research, and the archive kept it while the work had no other.

The title moved three times before it settled.

- In mid August, *2047: Arquivo de depois*. The archive of after.
- On August 22, with the new master instruction for the writing engine, *2047: Arquivos de Antes do Depois*.
- In September the order turned around and stayed. **2047: Arquivos do depois de antes**. Archives of the after of before. Everything is the before of some after, and the inversion is the point. The title was fixed in September and confirmed again in October by both authors.

The title is the same in Portuguese and in English. The English version of the site keeps it untranslated. Any gloss in English, like the one above, is a reading aid and not a second title.

This repository was renamed to match. The old address redirects.

## Herética Ablation

The same master instruction of August 22 gave the book a narrator with a name, **Herética Ablation**, a synthetic narrator created by Giselle Beiguelman and Felipe Sztutman.

Herética is a narrator-archive, a structural character implicated in what the book tells. The narrator writes from after the events and knows the archive was built from work taken without asking, and does not stand outside it. At the time of the naming the conceptual convergence was literal. The writing engine was a local "heretic" model, an open model with its refusals removed, and the narrator's material origin was the apparatus itself. The production machinery became the spine of the fiction. The site that holds the novel carries the name, heretica.xyz.

Two authorial rules came with the name.

- Herética is the author of the book and not of the preface. The preface speaks in the voice of the two artists.
- The name stays even though the writing moved, in September, from a local model to a cloud model (see `2026-09-restart-from-zero-and-the-2047-structure.md`). The contradiction between a heretical name and a commercial infrastructure is not hidden. It became authorial material.

## What this changes in the archive

New notes use the title. Older notes keep *Projeto 2047 / Arquivo Terra Viva* where they were written under it, because the archive records the process and does not rewrite it.
