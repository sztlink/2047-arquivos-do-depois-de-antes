# Field note, July 2026: the portal goes live and the voice settles

Two months separate this note from the May smoke tests. They contained the project's two biggest steps so far: the machine gained a public body, and the novel gained a voice.

## The portal is live

The machine's public surfaces are online at **<https://2047.szt.link>**: sources, triage, desk, archive, projection. The reading loop runs three times a day against the curated source basket, and everything it collects waits for human triage. The portal is the work's control room made public: visitors see the machine listening to the present, not a finished product.

## Living curation and the autonomy ladder

The curation model matured into something worth documenting on its own: new sources collect before they are approved (approval informed by the harvest), evidence is computed live, and every human decision is recorded with its reason as training material for a measured, revocable autonomy. See `../02-generative-system/curation-autonomy-ladder.md`.

## From fragments to a continuous novel

The work no longer writes reactive fragments. It writes one continuous novel set in 2047, with a persistent cast and an arc that advances, the news metabolized as pressure inside the story. See `../02-generative-system/continuous-novel.md`.

## The voice settles

The voice was built empirically: a cleared corpus from the co-author, a profile separating what crosses to fiction from what stays essay, five dosage versions of the opening chapter, a co-authorial reading, and a lapidation pass harvested edit by edit. The memory-inverted voice won: the novel is narrated from later inside 2047. The finest calibration turned out to be punctuation, settled from the co-author's own marks on the page. See `../02-generative-system/voice-construction-method.md`.

The first chapters exist. The text itself is reserved for the paper archive and the work's own formats; this repository documents the process, not the novel.

## The visual language

The portal was redesigned around a set of image references: control-room structure, warm editorial temperature, charcoal and ember, monospaced throughout. Two generative surfaces came out of it:

- The desk triangle (extracted territory, processing power, buying market) became a pure WebGL2 particle system, tens of thousands of GPU-simulated particles whose flow renders the triangle as behavior instead of ornament. Form emerges from behavior; the diagram is alive because the dispute it describes is alive.
- A live-listening prototype (the apparatus as performance: real collected items as embers in a field of dust) exists behind a flag, pending co-authorial decision on whether it becomes public.

The image language of the printed and projected work remains an open study.

## Resilience

The machine currently runs on ateliê infrastructure. A migration to a dedicated, resilient host is planned and staged, so that a long-duration work does not depend on a single domestic point of failure.
