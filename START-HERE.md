# Start here

If you have five minutes, start here.

## What this is

**2047: Arquivos do depois de antes** is an artwork by Giselle Beiguelman and Felipe Sztutman, on view at Casa Firjan, Rio de Janeiro, from October 15 to November 16, 2026. The work online is at <https://heretica.xyz>.

This repository is the public, sanitized research archive for Felipe Sztutman's technical-artistic layer of the process: apparatus, printer, video wall, generative system, hardware body, e-waste, and archive policy.

It is not an official institutional repository, not a press release, and not a production folder.

## Short thesis

```txt
The machine does not produce the future.
It reveals the present being mined.
```

The project treats AI as material infrastructure, not immaterial intelligence. It asks how extractive computation, rare earths, e-waste, obsolete media, and archive politics can become an installation apparatus.

## The apparatus in one paragraph

A collector reads news of the present and files each item under one of four vertices of the rare-earth and datacenter chain. A writing engine turns one item into one chapter of a novel set in 2047, narrated by Herética Ablation, a synthetic narrator that knows what it was built from. In the room, an Epson LQ-590 dot-matrix printer types the chapter as a full page on continuous paper, at the pace of the needle, with a QR code to the chapter online and a declared cost at the foot of the page: tokens of generation, watt-hours of video measured at the GPU, watt-hours of printing measured at the socket. A video wall plays two scenes per chapter, generated locally, and captions the sentence the needle is writing. Twenty-three chapters fill one day. The novel ends at chapter 2047.

## Why a dot-matrix printer

The printer is not nostalgia. It is a bureaucratic machine of flow, record, noise, and institutional truth. In this work, it becomes a witness: a device that prints a future already embedded in the material conditions of the present. Its noise is part of the work, so its quiet mode stays off.

## Why e-waste

The project refuses the fantasy that computation is clean or immaterial. AI depends on energy, water, minerals, supply chains, data centers, networks, and discarded hardware. E-waste is not decoration here. It is evidence. The declared cost on every page is the same argument made at the scale of one chapter.
