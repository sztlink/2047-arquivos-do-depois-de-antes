# Notice

2047: Arquivos do depois de antes is an artwork by Giselle Beiguelman and Felipe Sztutman, on view at Casa Firjan, Rio de Janeiro, from October 15 to November 16, 2026. Until August 2026 it ran under the working title Projeto 2047 / Arquivo Terra Viva.

This repository is a public, sanitized research archive maintained by Felipe Sztutman. It documents the technical-artistic apparatus and research process. It is not an official institutional repository, production folder, or exhibition announcement. The work itself is online at https://heretica.xyz.

No private conversations, institutional documents, contracts, budgets, character sheets, or unpublished third-party materials should be committed here.
